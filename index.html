📄 Transport Service Agreement

Please read the full agreement below before accepting.

Parties: SpaceKidz (Pty) Ltd, which operates SpaceKidz Preschool and Cathkin Academy, 7 Cathkin Street, Ravenswood, Boksburg (“the Provider”), and the Parent(s)/Guardian(s) who complete and sign this form (“the Parent”).

1. The Transport Service

1.1 The Provider agrees to transport the child/children named in this form between their school and the drop-off address given in this form, on the days and at the times confirmed by the Provider.

1.2 This is a transport-only agreement. It does not enrol the child at SpaceKidz Preschool, the Cathkin Academy Homework Centre, or any aftercare programme.

1.3 The service is offered subject to available seats and only for routes in areas near the Provider. Before the service starts, the Provider will confirm the route, the estimated collection and drop-off times, and the monthly transport fee in writing (WhatsApp or email). The service starts only once this has been confirmed and the first payment has been received.

1.4 Collection and drop-off times are estimates. Traffic, weather, road closures, traffic-light outages, delays at school, or vehicle breakdowns may cause delays. The Provider will keep the Parent informed where reasonably possible, but is not liable for delays beyond its reasonable control.

2. Collection from School

2.1 The child must be at the agreed collection point at the agreed time. The driver will wait a maximum of 5 minutes and will try to contact the Parent. If the child still cannot be found, the driver may continue with the route, the Parent will be informed, and the child remains in the care of the school and the Parent.

2.2 The Parent must notify the Provider on WhatsApp (082 539 8814) by 12:00 on the day if the child is absent, will be collected by someone else, has an extra-mural activity or detention, or will be dismissed at a different time.

2.3 On early-dismissal days, the Provider will try to accommodate the earlier time but cannot guarantee it. The Parent must notify the Provider of early dismissals as soon as the school announces them.

2.4 The Provider will not hand the child to anyone else at school, or change the route or drop-off address, without written instruction from the Parent.

3. Drop-off at Home

3.1 The child will be handed over only to the Parent or to a person listed as authorised to receive the child in this form. The driver may ask that person for proof of identity.

3.2 If no authorised person is available to receive the child within 5 minutes, and the Parent cannot be reached, the child will be taken back to the Provider at 7 Cathkin Street, Ravenswood, and the Parent must collect the child there. A reasonable additional trip fee and aftercare fee may be charged, and a late-collection fee of R100 per half hour (or part thereof) applies after closing time (Monday–Thursday 17:30, Friday 17:00).

3.3 Unaccompanied drop-off: Only where the Parent has selected this option in this form, the driver will drop the child at the drop-off address and wait until the child has entered the property. The Provider's responsibility for the child ends once the child has entered the property, and the Parent accepts full responsibility for the child from that moment.

3.4 Any change to the drop-off address requires 48 hours' written notice and the Provider's approval, and may change the monthly fee.

4. Safety & Conduct in the Vehicle

4.1 The Provider will use roadworthy vehicles and drivers who hold a valid driver's licence and Professional Driving Permit (PrDP).

4.2 Children must remain seated with their seatbelts fastened for the entire trip.

4.3 Children must treat the driver, other children, and the vehicle with respect. Fighting, bullying, swearing, throwing objects, distracting the driver, or opening doors or windows without permission is not allowed.

4.4 Where a child's behaviour is unsafe, the Parent will be informed. Should unsafe behaviour continue, the Provider may suspend or end the service with no refund of fees paid.

4.5 The Parent is liable for the cost of repairing any damage to the vehicle caused deliberately or carelessly by the child.

4.6 The Provider is not responsible for belongings, cell phones, or valuables lost or left in the vehicle.

5. Health & Emergencies

5.1 The Parent must disclose any medical condition, allergy, or special need that the driver should be aware of, and must update the Provider if this changes.

5.2 Drivers do not give children any medication.

5.3 In an emergency, the Provider is authorised to obtain necessary medical attention for the child if the Parent cannot be reached. The Parent accepts responsibility for all medical expenses incurred.

6. Fees & Payment

6.1 The monthly transport fee is the amount confirmed by the Provider in writing for the child's route and days of travel.

6.2 Fees are payable in advance by the last day of the preceding month. The first month's fee must be paid before the service starts. Please use the child's name and surname as the payment reference.

6.3 Payments received after the 5th of the month attract an automatic R220 late-payment penalty.

6.4 If fees are not paid, the service will be suspended until the account is up to date.

6.5 The monthly fee is a fixed amount that already allows for school and public holidays. It is not reduced for holidays, absences, school events, or days the service is not used. No fee is charged for a full calendar month in which the Provider provides no transport.

6.6 Should any account become overdue and be handed over for collection, the Parent agrees to be liable for all collection costs, tracing fees, commission charged by the debt collection agency (Accountability, the Provider's appointed agent), legal costs on the attorney-and-client scale where applicable, and interest on overdue amounts at the maximum rate permitted by law under the Prescribed Rate of Interest Act 55 of 1975. These costs are added to and payable as part of the outstanding account, in addition to the amount originally owed.

6.7 Transport fees may be adjusted (for example, after fuel price increases) with a minimum of 30 days' written notice.

7. Duration & Cancellation

7.1 Where the Parent has chosen an ongoing service, this Agreement continues month to month until either party ends it.

7.2 Where the Parent has chosen transport for a specific period only, this Agreement ends automatically on the end date given in this form.

7.3 The Parent must give one full calendar month's written notice to end an ongoing service. Failure to give notice results in liability for one month's fee in lieu of notice.

7.4 The Provider may end the service with two weeks' written notice, or immediately in the case of non-payment, persistent unsafe behaviour by the child, or abusive conduct by a Parent or authorised person toward the driver or staff.

8. Liability & Indemnity

8.1 The Provider and its drivers will care for the child to the best of their ability while the child is in their care, which begins when the child enters the vehicle and ends when the child is handed over (clause 3.1) or enters the property (clause 3.3).

8.2 The Provider will not accept liability for any accident, injury, loss, or damage arising during the service, except in cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct. The Parent indemnifies the Provider and its drivers against any claims that may arise in respect of the child, save where South African law provides otherwise.

9. POPIA Compliance

The Parent consents to the Provider collecting and processing their and their child's personal information, in accordance with POPIA, for the purpose of providing the transport service, administration, and communication. Names, contact numbers, and the drop-off address will be shared with the driver only as needed to provide the service.

10. General

This Agreement is binding on both parties and governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa. Amendments must be in writing and accepted by both the Provider and the Parent. Written notice may be given by email or WhatsApp.
